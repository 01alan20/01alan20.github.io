The growth story

The page follows a simple path from raw data to decisions, then turns the decision points into tests a team can actually run.

  1. Raw GA4 events

    Start with event rows, nested parameters, product arrays, and daily BigQuery export tables.

  2. Session/item marts

    Rebuild sessions and product interactions so the analysis matches how customers move.

  3. Funnel and revenue metrics

    Measure where attention becomes cart intent, checkout, purchase, and revenue.

  4. Segment associations

    Compare channels, products, devices, countries, and behavior depth against the baseline.

  5. Business actions

    Choose where to fix friction, protect revenue, increase visibility, or pause spend.

  6. Experiment backlog

    Turn the findings into hypotheses with segments, metrics, and measurement plans.

What the team should do next

The live BigQuery output points to three practical moves: fix the biggest funnel loss, separate product visibility from product conversion, and treat channel quality as a budget decision.

What I would test first

Experiment backlog items should be specific enough that a team can assign an owner, pick a metric, and know what BigQuery query will judge the result.
