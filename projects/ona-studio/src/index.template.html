About the dataset & key findings+

Understanding the consulting network

An anonymized sample of 46 employees. Information and value relationships are separate, directed nomination networks with 1–5 rating fields. The original dataset does not define the rating-scale labels, organizational codes, communication volume, or time spent collaborating.

773

Relationships in both layers

773 directed pairs appear in both networks; 104 appear only in Information and 82 only in Value.

45.6%

Relationships crossing communities

437 of 959 distinct directed pairs connect people in different algorithmically identified communities.

109 vs 86

Different directions, different coverage

The combined layer records 109 nominations from Community B to C and 86 from C to B.

Figures above use the combined network and are fixed case-study findings. Dashboard selections change the interactive charts and rankings. Communities reflect network structure, not verified departments. Measures describe reported ties, not observed workload or employee performance.
